Business Analysis Report: Marketing Campaign Performance Analysis

Executive Summary

The objective of this analysis was to evaluate the effectiveness of approximately 10,000 marketing campaigns executed across five marketing channels- Display, Influencer, Email, Search, and Social.

The analysis focused on campaign profitability, customer acquisition efficiency, return on advertising investment, conversion performance, and seasonal marketing trends. SQL was used for data preparation and business analysis, while Power BI was used to create an interactive dashboard for executive decision-making.

Overall, the organization achieved a healthy average ROAS of approximately 2.0 and generated over $25.5 million in profit from approximately $51 million in revenue. Performance across channels remained relatively balanced, although Display advertising consistently produced the highest revenue contribution while Influencer campaigns demonstrated the lowest acquisition costs.
1. Which marketing channel generates the highest revenue?

Findings

Display advertising generated the highest overall revenue among all five channels.

Channel | Revenue Share |
---------- | ------------- |
Display | ~21.0%        |
Influencer| ~20.7%        |
Email | ~19.9%        |
Search | ~19.4%        |
Social | ~19.1%        |

Business Insight

Although the revenue distribution is relatively balanced across channels, Display campaigns maintain a slight but consistent lead.

This indicates that Display advertising remains the strongest revenue driver and should continue receiving strategic investment while monitoring its efficiency.


2. Which marketing channel generates the highest profit?

Findings

Display advertising also generated the highest total profit.

Profit contribution:

* Display ≈ 21.1%
* Influencer ≈ 20.7%
* Email ≈ 19.9%
* Search ≈ 19.5%
* Social ≈ 18.8%

Business Insight

Since Display leads in both revenue and profitability, it appears to deliver sustainable financial returns rather than simply generating high sales volume.


3. Which channel delivers the best Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)?

Findings

Average ROAS across all channels remained remarkably consistent.

| Channel    | Average ROAS |
| ---------- | ------------ |
| Search     | 2.01         |
| Email      | 2.01         |
| Display    | 2.00         |
| Influencer | 2.00         |
| Social     | 1.99         |

Business Insight

Every marketing channel returns approximately two dollars for every dollar invested.

No single channel dramatically outperforms the others in advertising efficiency.

This suggests that marketing investments are already fairly optimized across channels.


4. Which channel has the lowest Customer Acquisition Cost (CPA)?

Findings

Influencer campaigns produced the lowest acquisition cost.

Average CPA:

* Influencer ≈ $9.11
* Email ≈ $10.13
* Social ≈ $10.33
* Display ≈ $10.80
* Search ≈ $10.89

### Business Insight

Influencer marketing acquires customers at the lowest cost.

This channel provides an opportunity to improve marketing efficiency while maintaining competitive revenue generation.


5. Which campaigns are the top revenue generators?

Findings

The highest-performing campaigns generated nearly $14.9K in revenue individually.

Examples include:

* CAMP01547
* CAMP04227
* CAMP04560
* CAMP05177
* CAMP03553

These campaigns also demonstrated:

* High ROAS (~3.0)
* Strong profitability
* High conversion counts

Business Insight

The top-performing campaigns share similar efficiency characteristics and should be studied to identify best practices that can be replicated across future marketing initiatives.


6. Which campaigns are the most profitable?

Findings

Top campaigns generated profits approaching $9.9K. Their profit margins consistently exceeded 66%, indicating highly efficient spending.

Business Insight

High profitability is driven not only by revenue generation but also by effective cost management.

These campaigns represent benchmarks for future campaign planning.


7. Which campaigns have the highest ROI?

Findings

Several campaigns achieved:

* ROI = 2.0
* ROAS = 3.0

These represent the strongest investment returns within the dataset.

Business Insight

Although multiple campaigns reached maximum ROI values, they belong to different channels, indicating that high-performing campaigns are not limited to a single marketing platform.


8. Which campaigns acquire customers most efficiently?

Findings

Several campaigns recorded exceptionally low CPA values.

For example:

* CAMP06220
* CAMP00757
* CAMP02044

These campaigns acquired customers at acquisition costs below $0.05.

Business Insight

These campaigns demonstrate excellent cost efficiency and should be analyzed to understand the targeting strategies responsible for such low acquisition costs.


9. Which campaigns produce the highest conversions?

Findings

Top campaigns generated between approximately 6,300–8,000 conversions.

Many of these belonged to the Influencer and Social channels.

Business Insight

High conversion campaigns indicate effective customer engagement and strong campaign messaging.

Future campaign design should replicate similar audience targeting and creative strategies.


10. How does marketing performance vary across months?

Findings

Monthly revenue remained relatively stable throughout the year.

No significant seasonal spikes or declines were observed.

Average ROAS also remained consistent across months.

Business Insight

Marketing performance appears evenly distributed throughout the calendar year.

This suggests that demand is relatively stable, allowing management to maintain consistent marketing budgets instead of concentrating spending during specific seasons.


11. Is there any relationship between revenue and profit?

Findings

The scatter plot demonstrates a strong positive relationship between revenue and profit.

Campaigns generating higher revenue also consistently generated higher profits.

Business Insight

Revenue growth directly contributes to profitability.

No major outliers were observed where revenue increased while profitability declined.


12. Which channel contributes the largest share of conversions?

Findings

Total conversions exceeded 10 million.

Influencer and Display campaigns contributed the largest conversion volumes.

Business Insight

These channels successfully convert customer interest into completed actions and should remain important components of the marketing mix.


13. Funnel Analysis

Findings

The marketing funnel demonstrates a significant decrease in customer volume across each stage:

Impressions
-Clicks
--Leads
---Conversions

The largest drop occurs immediately after impressions.

Business Insight

Improving click-through performance presents the greatest opportunity for increasing total conversions.

Enhancing advertisement quality, targeting precision, and creative content could substantially improve overall campaign effectiveness.


14. Overall Business Conclusions

Key Findings

* Display is the highest revenue and profit generating channel.
* Influencer marketing achieves the lowest acquisition costs.
* Search and Email provide the highest ROAS.
* Revenue is distributed relatively evenly across all marketing channels.
* Marketing performance remains stable throughout the year.
* Revenue and profit exhibit a strong positive relationship.
* Top-performing campaigns consistently demonstrate high ROAS, strong profitability, and efficient customer acquisition.


Recommendations

1. Continue investing in Display campaigns due to their consistent leadership in revenue and profitability.

2. Increase investment in Influencer marketing to capitalize on its low customer acquisition costs.

3. Study the top-performing campaigns to identify successful targeting strategies, messaging, and budget allocation practices.

4. Improve click-through rates by optimizing advertisement creatives, audience segmentation, and campaign messaging.

5. Maintain a diversified marketing strategy since all five channels contribute meaningful revenue without excessive dependence on a single platform.
